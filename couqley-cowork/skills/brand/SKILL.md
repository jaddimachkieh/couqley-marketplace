# Couqley French Bistro — Brand Guidelines

This skill ensures all materials created for Couqley French Bistro follow the established brand identity, visual standards, and communication tone. Based on the 2017 Brand Profile.

## When to Use This Skill

Use whenever creating any output for Couqley:
- Social media posts, captions, stories
- Marketing reports and dashboards
- Promotional materials and campaign designs
- Email newsletters and customer communications
- Menu descriptions and food photography captions
- Internal reports and presentations
- Financial presentations, scenario projections, investor materials
- Any HTML, PDF, PPTX, or DOCX deliverable

## Brand Identity

### Brand Name
**Couqley French Bistro**
- Exact capitalization: "Couqley" (not "COUQLEY" in body text)
- Tagline: "FRENCH BISTRO" (caps, beneath the main logo)

### Brand Positioning
Authentic French bistro — classic cuisine meets warm hospitality. Sophisticated without being pretentious.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Cream Background | #F7F3E9 | (247, 243, 233) | Primary backgrounds, cards |
| Couqley Red | #CC3333 | (204, 51, 51) | Logo, headers, emphasis, CTAs |
| Gold/Tan Accent | #BF9966 | (191, 153, 102) | Borders, decorative elements |

### Chart & Data Colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Dark Red | #B42D2D | (180, 45, 45) | Negative/Bear scenario |
| Medium Tan | #D2B48C | (210, 180, 140) | Base/neutral data |
| Light Tan | #E4D2B4 | (228, 210, 180) | Target/positive data |

### Usage Rules
- Cream dominates as primary background
- Red used sparingly for impact (logos, key headers, CTAs)
- Gold frames and accents content without overwhelming
- High contrast maintained for readability

## Typography

### Font Hierarchy
- **Display Headers**: 36-48pt, Bold, Couqley Red or Gold
- **Section Headers**: 24-30pt, Bold, ALL CAPS
- **Subheaders**: 18-20pt, Bold
- **Body Text**: 11-12pt, Regular, serif or clean sans-serif
- **Captions/Social**: 9-10pt, Regular or Italic

### Rules
- Classic serif or elegant sans-serif for body
- ALL CAPS for "FRENCH BISTRO" tagline
- ALL CAPS for major section headers
- Dark grey/black for body text on cream

## Logo Usage

**Components:**
1. "COUQLEY" wordmark in red
2. "FRENCH BISTRO" tagline with decorative flourishes
3. Elegant gold border frames

**Placement:** Top right on content pages, centered on covers/titles

**Don'ts:** No stretching, no color changes outside palette, no removing tagline, no effects

## Tone of Voice

### Marketing & Social Media
- Warm, inviting, authentic
- Sophisticated but approachable
- French terminology used sparingly (not every post)
- Focus on sensory experiences (taste, aroma, ambiance)
- Conversational yet refined

**Good:** "Slow-braised, richly flavored, and served with a glass of something wonderful — our Coq au Vin is a love letter to French comfort."

**Bad:** "TRY OUR AMAZING COQ AU VIN! BEST IN TOWN! ORDER NOW!"

### Professional/Financial
- Data-driven, precise
- Confident in projections
- Lead with business impact, not methodology

### Internal
- Clear, concise
- Same brand tone consistency
- Professional but friendly

## Design Elements

### Borders & Frames
- Curved-corner frames in gold (#BF9966)
- Double-line borders for formal documents
- Subtle decorative flourishes at corners

### Layout Principles
- Clean, spacious layouts with generous white space
- Symmetrical, balanced compositions
- Classical, elegant aesthetic
- Never cluttered

## Data Visualization

### Number Formatting
- Currency: $1,935,292 (commas, no decimals unless cents)
- Percentages: 28% (whole numbers unless precision needed)
- Large numbers: full with commas, no abbreviations

### Table Standards
- Header row: white text on Couqley Red background
- Data rows: black text on cream/white
- Right-align numbers, left-align labels
- Minimal borders or gold accent borders

### Chart Standards
- Brand colors only
- Clear axis labels
- Data values shown on charts when space permits
- Legend at bottom
- Consistent style across all visualizations

## Financial Reporting Standards

Use when creating financial presentations, scenario projections, EBITDA reports, or investor materials.

### Scenario Analysis Format

**Structure:**
1. Use clear scenario labeling: "Base", "Target", "Bear"
2. Present 5-year projections (Year 1 through Year 5 plus Total)
3. Include both absolute values and percentages
4. All figures in USD

**Revenue & EBITDA:**
- Display in clear tabular format with progression across years
- Show Revenues, EBITDA (dollar amounts), and EBITDA % for each scenario and year
- Maintain consistent percentage formatting (e.g., 28%, 27%, 26%)

### Financial Presentations (PowerPoint/Keynote)

**Title slide:** Centered logo, cream background, gold border frame, document title, subtitle/date
**Content slides:** Logo top right, section headers in Couqley Red on red background, body on cream
**Financial slides:** Clean tabular layouts, red headers for tables, consistent number formatting, charts using brand palette
**Footer:** Company name, date, page numbers

### Financial Documents (Word/PDF)

**Header:** Logo (top right or centered), document title, date and version
**Body:** Professional formatting, brand color accents in headers, tables following scenario format above
**Footer:** Page numbers, confidentiality statements if needed, company name

### Technical Specifications

- Presentations: .pptx, 16:9 ratio
- Documents: .docx or .pdf, Letter (8.5" × 11") or A4
- Images: .png, 300 dpi minimum
- Logo minimum size: 1 inch width
- Margins: 1 inch on all sides for documents

### Financial Content Checklist

- [ ] Scenarios (Base, Target, Bear) clearly labeled
- [ ] Numbers formatted with commas, percentages consistent
- [ ] Charts use brand colors (Bear: Dark Red, Base: Medium Tan, Target: Light Tan)
- [ ] Data labels clear and readable

## Accessibility

- Red on Cream: 4.8:1 contrast (WCAG AA compliant)
- White on Red: 6.2:1 contrast (WCAG AA compliant)
- Black on Cream: 12.6:1 contrast (WCAG AAA compliant)
- Minimum 11pt body text
- Charts have both color and pattern differentiation
