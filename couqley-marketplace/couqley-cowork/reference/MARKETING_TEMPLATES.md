# Couqley Marketing Content Templates

Reusable templates for social media, email, and promotional content. Customize with specific menu items, dates, and offers while maintaining Couqley's brand voice: elegant, French-inspired, warm, and inviting.

## Email Templates

- `[Week/Month] at Couqley: New Specials + [Seasonal Theme]`

```

[SECTION 1: Featured Item]

Price: [PRICE]

[Special date/time, offer, and tone]
```

## Brand Voice Guidelines

All content should reflect Couqley's personality:

- **Elegant, not snobbish:** Sophisticated but welcoming
- **French-inspired:** Reference technique, tradition, and heritage
- **Warmth:** Emphasize community, gathering, celebration
- **Quality-focused:** Details matter, ingredients matter, technique matters
- **Accessible:** Fine dining that's approachable, not intimidating

**Tone Examples:**
- ✅ "Slow-braised, richly flavored, and absolutely worth every bite."
- ✅ "Join us for an unforgettable evening."
- ❌ "Our food is the best in town."
- ❌ "You gotta try this omg!!!"

## Quick Template Remix Checklist

Before posting, verify:
- [ ] Brand colors match (Cream #F7F3E9, Red #CC3333, Gold #BF9966)
- [ ] Spelling and grammar are perfect
- [ ] Links and CTAs are clickable and correct
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant (3-10 per post)
- [ ] Tone aligns with Couqley voice
- [ ] Image quality is high-resolution
- [ ] Posted at optimal time for engagement (typically 6-9 PM weekdays, 11 AM-1 PM weekends)
- [ ] Call-to-action is clear
